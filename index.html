LinkedIn Outreach Manual

A practical guide to reaching Indian coaches on LinkedIn.

Step-by-Step Warm-Up Strategy

Step 1 · Profile Optimization

Make sure your LinkedIn profile is complete, professional, and clearly says what value you bring.

Step 2 · Initial Engagement

Pick 3–5 recent posts from your target coach. Like them and leave thoughtful, specific comments.

Step 3 · Share & Endorse

Share one of their posts with your network and endorse them for skills you genuinely respect.

Step 4 · View Profile

After a few days of engagement, view their profile — the notification does the introducing.

Message Templates

1 · Connection Request
Dear [Coach's Name],

I've been following your work on LinkedIn and find your perspective genuinely valuable.

I help coaches amplify their impact through strategic Instagram growth.

Would you be open to connecting?

Warm regards,
[Your Name]
2 · Follow-up Message
Dear [Coach's Name],

Thanks for connecting! I've been impressed by [mention something specific from their profile].

I specialize in helping coaches grow their presence on Instagram. Many impactful coaches struggle to turn Instagram into a lead channel — I share actionable tips tailored to your niche, no obligation attached.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
3 · Soft Pitch
Dear [Coach's Name],

I've taken a closer look at your Instagram presence and noticed the incredible work you're doing.

One small tweak could turn your profile into a steady source of discovery calls. Happy to share what I see — no pitch, just an outside eye.

Would that be useful?

[Your Name]

LinkedIn Sales Navigator Tips

  • Advanced Search Filters — use Industry, Job Title, Geography, Seniority Level, and Follower Count filters.
  • Lead Lists — create a custom lead list per niche for organized tracking.
  • Account Insights — study company pages to understand content strategy.
  • Real-time Alerts — set alerts for saved leads' new posts and job changes.
